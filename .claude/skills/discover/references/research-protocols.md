# Research Protocols

## Research Depth by Scale

| Scale | Searches per Checkpoint |
|---|---|
| Quick Build | 1-3 |
| Standard | 3-8 |
| Platform | 8-15 |
| Pioneering | 10-20 |

## 6 Research Checkpoints

| # | When | What | Modes |
|---|---|---|---|
| RC1 | After Phase 2 (Core Identity) | Context/landscape research | All |
| RC2 | After Phase 3A (Deep Dive) | Feature-level best practices | Guided+ |
| RC3 | After Phase 3B (Edge Cases) | UX/interaction patterns | Guided+ |
| RC4 | During Phase 4 (Assumptions) | Assumption validation | Guided+ |
| RC5 | During Phase 5 (Dimensions) | Stack + architecture | All |
| RC6 | Phase Transition Review | Market refresh | Phase Transition |

## Research Protocol Template (all archetypes follow this)

1. Define 3-5 specific search queries from the archetype section below
2. Execute searches via WebSearch
3. Extract relevant insight per result
4. Synthesize into 3-5 sentence briefing
5. Flag findings that challenge assumptions
6. Ask: "Does this change anything?"
7. Log to `docs/research/discover-{topic}.md`
