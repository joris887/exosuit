# Archetype: Experiential / Entertainment

**Core Question:** "What should someone FEEL?"
**Examples:** Games, interactive art, music visualizers, simulations, creative coding experiments

## Phase 2: Core Identity Questions

**Q1:** "What should someone FEEL when using this?"
Scaffold — present MDA aesthetics:
- "Wow, that's beautiful" (Sensation)
- "Can I beat this?" (Challenge)
- "What's over there?" (Discovery)
- "What happens next?" (Narrative)
- "I can create my own thing" (Expression)
- "Let's do this together" (Fellowship)
- "I'm someone/somewhere else" (Fantasy)
- "I just want to unwind" (Submission)
"Pick 1-3 most important."

**Q2:** "What are your 3 design pillars?"
Scaffold: "Non-negotiable essences. Mario Kart: 'Accessible fun, Competitive chaos, Pick-up-and-play'. Journey: 'Wordless connection, Emotional landscape, Effortless movement'. Your 3?"

**Q3:** "Describe the peak 10 seconds."
Scaffold: "The best moment someone could have. What are they seeing? Hearing? Doing? How do they feel?"

**Q4:** "What's the core loop?"
Scaffold: "The repeating cycle. Tetris: see→decide→place→clear. Minecraft: explore→gather→craft→build. Yours?"

**Q5:** "Name 3 reference projects. What do you love about each?"

## Phase 3: Deep Dive

**Reference deep dive:**
For each reference project: research it, ask what to borrow + what to change.

**Minimum viable experience:**
"48 hours, ONE thing — what's the minimum version that captures the feeling?"

**Jesse Schell's Toy Test:**
"Is this fun to interact with even without a goal? If the core mechanic isn't satisfying as a toy, adding objectives won't fix it."

**Difficulty/progression curve:**
Scaffold: "Flat (toy — no progression), Rising (game — gets harder), Branching (sim — player chooses complexity)?"

## Pre-Mortem Failure Scenarios

- "The core loop isn't engaging after 2 minutes"
- "Performance is too poor on target devices"
- "It's technically impressive but not fun"
- "The difficulty curve is too steep or too flat"
- "People don't understand what to do without instructions"
- "The art/audio quality doesn't meet expectations"
