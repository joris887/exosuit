# Archetype: Uncategorized / Hybrid

**Core Question:** "Tell me more and I'll help classify."
**Examples:** Novel concepts, AI-native apps, pioneering ideas, unusual combinations

## When This Applies

- User selected "None of these" during archetype classification
- The concept genuinely doesn't fit any of the 10 archetypes
- The concept is too novel to classify without exploration

## Phase 2: Freeform Exploration

**Q1:** "In 3-4 sentences, paint the picture. What does someone experience when they use this?"
Scaffold: "Don't describe features — describe the experience. 'A person opens their laptop, types a question, and...' What happens?"

**Q2:** "What's genuinely new here — something that doesn't exist yet?"
Scaffold: "New combination of existing things? New technology applied to an old problem? Something never tried before? All are valid."

**Q3:** "What's the hardest unsolved problem in this concept?"
Scaffold: "The thing that might not work. The technical risk, the user behavior assumption, the 'can we actually do this?' question."

**Q4:** "If you had to bet on one thing that might not work, what is it?"
Scaffold: "The single biggest risk. This becomes the first spike to validate."

## Phase 3: Deep Research

For uncategorized concepts, research is the primary tool:
- Search academic papers for similar concepts
- Search patents for adjacent technology
- Search GitHub for experimental implementations
- Search Hacker News / Reddit for discussions of similar ideas
- Search for the underlying technology stack feasibility

## After Research: Reclassify or Route to Pioneering

With better understanding from research, attempt to reclassify:
- "Based on what we've learned, this is closest to [archetype] + [archetype]. Want to continue with that framing?"
- If still unclassifiable: route to Pioneering mode (spike-first discovery)

## Pre-Mortem Failure Scenarios

Generate 4-6 project-specific scenarios based on the concept (no generic list — derive from Q3 and Q4 answers).

**Pioneering additions:**
- "Core technical assumption is proven wrong by spike"
- "Concept works technically but users don't understand it"
- "Novel component is too slow for real-time use"
- "Well-funded competitor announces the same idea"
