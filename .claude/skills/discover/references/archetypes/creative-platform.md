# Archetype: Creative Expression Platform

**Core Question:** "What can people MAKE?"
**Examples:** Canva, GarageBand, Scratch, level editors, pixel art tools

## Phase 2: Core Identity Questions

**Q1:** "Show me an example output — what would someone CREATE with this?"
Scaffold: "Describe (or sketch) the artifact someone would produce. A graphic? A song? A game level? A 3D model?"

**Q2:** "What creative ability does this unlock?"
Scaffold: "Canva: non-designers can make professional graphics. GarageBand: non-musicians can make songs. What previously-hard thing becomes easy?"

**Q3:** "30-second creation test — what's the simplest thing someone creates immediately?"
Scaffold: "The first win. Canva: pick a template and change the text. What's yours? If it takes more than 30 seconds to make SOMETHING, the onboarding needs work."

**Q4:** "What constraints make it powerful?"
Scaffold: "Twitter's character limit forced concision. Instagram's square format created an aesthetic. Minecraft's blocks enabled infinity. What deliberate limitation unlocks creativity?"

**Q5:** "How do people share or showcase what they've made?"
Scaffold: "Export as image/file? Gallery within the app? Social media? Embed on other sites? Print?"

## Phase 3: Deep Dive

**Creation workflow walkthrough:**
"Walk me through creating something start to finish. What tools are available? What decisions does the creator make?"

**Template/starting point strategy:**
"Do people start from blank, from templates, or from examples? How many templates at launch?"

**Save/export/share pipeline:**
"How is the creation stored? Auto-save? Manual save? Export formats? Can someone share a link to edit?"

## Pre-Mortem Failure Scenarios

- "The blank canvas problem — people don't know where to start"
- "Creations look amateurish despite the tool"
- "The learning curve is too steep for the target audience"
- "Export quality doesn't meet expectations"
- "No one shares their creations"
- "Power users outgrow it quickly"
