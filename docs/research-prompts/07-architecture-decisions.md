# 7. Architecture Decision Records (ADRs)

## Research Prompt

```
I need deep research on Architecture Decision Records — how to document, govern, and leverage architectural decisions in software projects. The goal is to determine the best possible ADR template and process that captures decisions in a way useful for both human teams and AI assistants.

**Framework context:** This template is part of the JD-LLM Development Framework — a language-agnostic AI development framework for Claude Code. ADRs are:
- Generated by /architecture-check when architectural drift is detected
- Discoverable by AI during /story-cycle planning (the AI searches ADRs before proposing implementation approaches)
- Used to prevent re-litigation of past decisions
- Linked to ground rules (some decisions become permanent rules)
The template must be low-friction enough that people actually write them, and structured enough that AI can find and respect relevant decisions.

**Research areas** (starting points — include anything significant you discover beyond these):

1. **ADR Formats & Evolution** — Michael Nygard's original, MADR v4, Y-statements, adr-tools/log4brains. Lightweight vs heavyweight. How Google, Spotify, ThoughtWorks use ADRs.

2. **What Makes an ADR Useful** — Research on adoption. What developers actually look for. How ADRs prevent re-litigation. Decision context preservation. ADR linking.

3. **Decision Governance** — Who writes/reviews. Status lifecycle. When a change warrants an ADR vs a commit message. ADR review in PRs. Compliance checking.

4. **ADR Anti-Patterns** — Decisions without alternatives explored. Written after the fact. Too many (noise) vs too few. Orphaned from code. Decisions made in Slack/meetings.

5. **ADRs for AI-Assisted Development** — Structuring ADRs so AI consults them during planning. Expressing "why NOT to do X." ADR + ground rules integration. Preventing AI from re-proposing rejected alternatives.

6. **ADR Automation & Tooling** — Auto-generating proposals. Compliance checking. Templates that reduce friction. Making decisions discoverable.

**Required output format:**
1. Executive summary
2. Per-topic findings with citations
3. **Recommended ADR format** — propose the specific fields, structure, and level of detail, with justification
4. **Recommended governance process** — who writes, who reviews, when, lifecycle
5. **Recommended AI integration approach** — how AI should discover and use ADRs
6. Tool comparisons
7. Knowledge gaps
```
